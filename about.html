About SAMMI E-Library

Last updated: [SEPTEMBER 2026]

SAMMI E-Library is the digital library portal for St. Anthony of Makati Montessori Inc. It brings together the school's physical book catalog, curated open educational resources, and the DepEd K to 12 curriculum in one place — searchable by students, teachers, and families, and managed by the school's librarian.

What this site offers

  • A searchable catalog of the school's physical books, including availability and shelf location
  • Curated learning links organized by Dewey Decimal category
  • DepEd MATATAG & K to 12 resources organized by grade level and Senior High School track
  • Open-access titles from public-domain and open-education libraries
