Privacy Notice

Last updated: [add date when you publish this]

This notice explains, specifically and accurately, what SAMMI E-Library collects, why, where it's stored, and who is able to see it. It covers every account type: students signing in with an LRN, and librarian/staff accounts.

What we collect, and who can see it

DataWhen it's collectedWho can see it
Learning Reference Number (LRN)Every time a student signs inNot shown to other students. Only librarian accounts can view login records.
Login date & timeEvery sign-inLibrarian accounts only
Which resource links you openEvery time you click a resource card (curriculum links, Dewey directory, open-access books, etc.)Librarian accounts only — used to see which resources are actually useful, never shown publicly or to other students
Borrower's LRN & due date, for physical book checkoutsWhen a librarian checks out a physical book to youLibrarian accounts only
Librarian's email address & passwordWhen a librarian account is created by the site administratorPassword is never visible to anyone, including us — it is hashed and managed directly by our database provider's authentication system (see below)
Publicly visible

The book catalog itself — titles, authors, categories, availability ("3 of 5 available"), shelf location — is visible to anyone using the site, including without logging in. No personal data appears here.

Never publicly visible

Your LRN, login history, resource-click history, and borrowing records are never visible to other students and are not publicly accessible on the internet. Reading them requires a real, password-authenticated librarian account, and the database itself enforces this rule — it isn't just hidden by the page's design.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not ask students for a name, password, email, or any contact information — only an LRN.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell or share any data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a managed database provided by Supabase, a third-party cloud database service, in the [region — check Project Settings in your Supabase dashboard, e.g. Singapore] region. Access to the underlying database is restricted by row-level security rules enforced on Supabase's servers, not only by this website's design.

A note on student sign-in

Signing in currently checks that an LRN is a valid 12-digit number — it does not yet verify the number against an official school enrollment list. This means the system trusts that students enter their own real LRN. If SAMMI later connects this to a verified student roster, this notice will be updated to reflect that.

How long we keep it

Login records, resource-click history, and borrowing records are kept indefinitely unless a librarian deletes them. There is currently no automatic deletion schedule. [Update this if the school sets a retention policy, e.g. "records older than 2 school years are deleted."]

Your rights

Under the Philippines' Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you — or a parent/guardian on behalf of a minor — may ask to:

  • Be informed about what personal data is collected and why (this notice)
  • Access the personal data held about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion or blocking of your data, where applicable
  • Object to certain processing of your data

To make any of these requests, email sammi.library.hub@gmail.com or see the Contact page.

Changes to this notice

If what this site collects or how it's used changes, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
